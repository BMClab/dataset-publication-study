## Where the materials came from

The [provenance manifest](provenance.json) records the internal source revision, path, source SHA-256, distributed SHA-256 and adaptations for each imported file. The data are BMClab benchmark observations. Reductions of network probes retain selected fields. Workstation paths, restricted draft identifiers in applicable records, and private replay instructions have been removed. References marked “historical source” identify files outside this selection. Third-party publications and project documentation are linked in the historical reports. They retain their original attribution. The source layout rationale also informed the new toolkit case (`framework/docs/repository_layout.md` and `framework/src/reader_data/README.md` at the manifest revision).

The source entry points were `storage_benchmark.py` for the early layouts, `run_reader_matrix.py` and `reader_benchmark.py` for the extended matrix, and the individual `zenodo_*_probe.py` scripts for public-host capabilities. For the matched suite, `lab_format_fixture.py` called the internal release builder on curated NPZ and STL inputs, `lab_format_run.py` launched workers, and `zenodo_ppds_read.py` and `zenodo_ppds_local.py` measured remote and downloaded inputs. These entry points and the prepared data remained in the research archive. The distributed method files document the matched encoder and queries, the environments and the independently runnable table renderer.
